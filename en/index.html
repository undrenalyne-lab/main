A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we begin.

My project isn’t fully defined yet. Is it too early?+

No. Our first conversation helps clarify your objective, audience and constraints. It also helps establish whether my approach suits your needs.

How are pricing and timing determined?+

They depend on the scope, channels and deliverables. A quote and timeline are agreed before work begins. This page creates no automatic commitment.

What role does AI play in the creative process?+

It can help explore, produce or organise. Creative decisions and content checks remain human. Tools are selected according to the project.

How do we stay in touch?+

An initial conversation helps define the project. We then agree on communication channels and check-ins, using WhatsApp or Google Meet as appropriate.
